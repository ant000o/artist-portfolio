Hi!

My name is Josefa, better known as Fefastin. I’m an independent digital artist who loves creating enchanting girls, charming boys, and soft, dreamy worlds you can almost step into. ✨

My journey with art began when I was little. I was always drawing on every piece of paper I could find, and with my family’s encouragement I started taking art classes. What began as a simple hobby slowly became something much more meaningful.

Over time I explored different styles and techniques until I eventually discovered digital art. At first it felt overwhelming, but with practice and curiosity I completely fell in love with it. 💻🎨

Today I’m still learning, growing, and dreaming up new ideas I can’t wait to bring to life.

P.S. Thank you to everyone who has supported and believed in me. Your encouragement means the world to me. ❤️
