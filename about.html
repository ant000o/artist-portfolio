Hi!

My name is Josefa, better known as Fefastin. I’m an independent digital artist who loves creating enchanting girls, charming boys, and soft, dreamy worlds you can almost step into. ✨

My journey with art began when I was little. My family quickly noticed that I was always scribbling on every piece of paper I could find—and according to them, I had “talent” (parents always say that, right? 😄). So my hobby was encouraged, and I started attending art classes as a child. What began as simple fun slowly became something much more serious and meaningful.

Through school, I kept choosing art whenever I could. Sketchbooks filled up, pencils got shorter, and my curiosity kept growing. Over time, I explored different techniques and styles, trying to figure out what truly felt like me. There were moments when life got busy and art had to take a step back, but the passion never really disappeared—it was always there, quietly waiting.

Eventually, I discovered digital art. At first, it felt strange and overwhelming. Nothing worked the way I expected, and I missed the familiar feeling of traditional tools. But instead of giving up, I kept experimenting, learning, and pushing myself. Slowly but surely, things started to click. And then… I completely fell in love with it. 💻🎨

Digital art opened a whole new world for me—endless colors, magical brushes, and the freedom to create without limits. I’m still learning every day, still growing, still dreaming up new ideas I can’t wait to bring to life.

So come along on this journey with me. There’s so much more to explore, and I’m just getting started. 💫

P.S. A huge thank you to everyone who has supported, encouraged, and believed in me. Your kind words, challenges, and inspiration mean more than you know. I wouldn’t be here without you. Truly, thank you from the bottom of my heart. ❤️🤗
